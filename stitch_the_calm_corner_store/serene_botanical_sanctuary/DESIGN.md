## Brand & Style
The design system expresses understated luxury, thoughtful slow-living, and tactile botanical warmth. Tailored for discerning consumers seeking intentional living, interior curation, and wellness rituals, the aesthetic balances serene editorial quietude with organic elegance.

The visual style blends **Editorial Warm Minimalism** with **Tactile Organic Materiality**. Every surface draws cues from sun-drenched linen, unglazed ceramic stoneware, poured soy wax, and lush foliage. Negative space is celebrated rather than filled, offering immediate respiratory calm through spacious typography pairings, quiet borders, and ambient light-filtered depths.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in sunlit nature, natural clay, and earthy flora:

- **Dominant Base Surface**: Warm Ivory (`#F8F5ED`) forms the primary canvas, replacing harsh digital whites with the warmth of handmade cotton paper.
- **Secondary Surface**: Soft Beige (`#E8DDCC`) provides soft tonal framing for cards, circular badges, and table vessels.
- **Primary Accent**: Deep Olive (`#48543A`) supplies authoritative, tranquil brand grounding for high-priority interactive states, key buttons, and prominent editorial headers.
- **Supporting Botanical**: Sage Green (`#A3AE91`) delivers soothing organic presence for secondary tags, subtle pill fills, and botanical highlights.
- **Warm Earth Texture**: Warm Taupe (`#B8A58D`) introduces ceramic and natural wood tonality for dividers, subtle borders, and secondary accents.
- **Typography & Structure**: Charcoal (`#292920`) delivers deep, organic contrast with softening qualities compared to pure black, maintaining maximum legibility across editorial prose.

## Typography
The system employs a classic editorial tension between high-contrast transitional serifs and warm, geometric modernist sans:

- **Headings (Playfair Display)**: Infuses editorial poetry, craft, and grace. Used for brand declarations, collection titles, and contemplative feature intros. Italicized treatments are encouraged for intimate, reflective secondary clauses.
- **Body & Labels (Manrope)**: Delivers clean, unfussy clarity that prevents the UI from becoming uncomfortably ornate. The wide aperture and geometric proportions balance the lyricism of the serif.
- **Eyebrows & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase Manrope with generous tracking (`0.18em`) flanked by thin ornamental rule dividers to evoke vintage botanical compendiums and luxury apothecary labeling.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid on desktop and a flexible 4-column structure on mobile devices, calibrated for unhurried browsing:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Outer margins of `3rem` (`48px`) allow photography to breathe. Sections use deliberate vertical breathing room (`space-xl` to `5rem`), maintaining generous negative space around narrative vignettes and product stills.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with compact `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Vertical stack rhythms shift to `space-lg` to prevent excessive scrolling while preserving the spacious aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is suggested through natural light and physical surface layering rather than digital shadows:

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved primarily through warm surface transitions. Base layers sit on Warm Ivory (`#F8F5ED`), while secondary platforms, trays, and interactive containment utilize Soft Beige (`#E8DDCC`) or tinted warm neutrals.
- **Sunlight Shadows**: Where elevation is required (such as sticky purchase bars, modals, or flyout baskets), apply diffused, low-opacity warm ambient shadows simulating soft sunlit casting: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(41, 41, 32, 0.07)`.
- **Delicate Rule Lines**: Structural isolation relies on hairline borders in Warm Taupe at 20-30% opacity (`rgba(184, 165, 141, 0.35)`), reinforcing the feel of archival stationery.

## Shapes
The shape language mirrors handcrafted earthenware, pebble contours, and organic botanical arches:

- **Containers & Surfaces**: Modest `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`) corners soften standard product cards and inputs while maintaining structured editorial poise.
- **Feature Medallions & Avatars**: True circular geometries (`50%` / rounded-full) are applied to scent-icon badges, icon containers, and botanical discovery nodes.
- **Hero & Curated Vignettes**: Gentle arch geometries (`rounded-t-full` or asymmetric top arches) are utilized on accent media cards to reflect curved wall shelves and greenhouse window silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Olive background (`#48543A`), Warm Ivory text (`#F8F5ED`), subtle pill curvature (`rounded-full` or `rounded-md`), minimum height of `48px`, generous lateral padding (`1.75rem`). Hover transitions smoothly to a deepened shade (`#3B4530`) with an organic easing curve.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Hairline border (`1px solid #48543A`), Charcoal or Deep Olive text, transparent or Warm Ivory fill. On hover, transitions to Soft Beige fill (`#E8DDCC`).
- **Text Link**: Charcoal text with a delicate under-rule in Sage Green (`#A3AE91`) positioned `4px` beneath the baseline.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Card**: Boundless or grounded in Soft Beige (`#E8DDCC`) with subtle `rounded-md` framing. Imagery occupies an airy `4:5` aspect ratio with warm ambient backdrops. Product title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/size in `body-sm`, and price in confident Charcoal.
- **Vignette Feature Card**: Layered composition where photographic plant and candle imagery slightly overlaps a tinted backdrop card, accompanied by centered Playfair Display narrative copy and an icon medallion.

### Chips & Badges
- **Scent & Botanical Chips**: Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`) filled with Soft Beige (`#E8DDCC`) or sheer Sage Green (`rgba(163, 174, 145, 0.2)`), featuring `label-sm` Charcoal typography with `0.5rem` horizontal spacing.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields**: Warm Ivory background, framed by a soft Warm Taupe border (`1px solid rgba(184, 165, 141, 0.5)`). Focused state features a crisp Deep Olive ring (`1.5px solid #48543A`). Placeholders appear in muted Charcoal at 50% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular or soft-square forms tinted in Deep Olive when checked, accented by a warm ivory indicator.

### Dividers & Ornamentation
- **Apothecary Rules**: Horizontal hairline separators (`1px solid rgba(184, 165, 141, 0.3)`) punctuated by a central bow-tie glyph, leaf motif, or tracked uppercase category indicator.
